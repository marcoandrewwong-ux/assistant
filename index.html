Personal Workspace Assistant

A private personal productivity integration for organizing documents and carrying out owner directed workflows with authorized Google services.

Depending on the permissions granted and the requested task, the integration can work with Google Drive files, Docs, Sheets, Calendar and Gmail. Access is limited to the account owner's authorized purposes. This is a personal tool, not a public signup service.

This public website contains app information only. It does not provide access to the assistant, connected accounts or private documents.

Privacy policy

For support, use the developer contact shown on the Google authorization screen. Do not post private documents or credentials in public repository discussions.
