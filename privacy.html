Privacy policy

Personal Workspace Assistant is a personal productivity integration operated for its account owner's use. This notice describes its handling of Google user data.

Data accessed and purpose

Depending on granted permissions and the task requested, the integration may access account identifiers, file metadata and content in Drive, Docs and Sheets, calendar events, and Gmail message metadata or content. This information is used to retrieve, organize, summarize or prepare information and carry out actions authorized by the owner. A granted permission does not imply every connected service is used for every task.

Processing and sharing

The assistant runs through locally managed software and uses external AI services. Relevant task content may be transmitted to the configured AI provider, including OpenAI, to fulfill the owner's request. Owner requested outputs may be stored in connected productivity services. Such providers process data under their applicable terms and privacy policies. This integration does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or intentionally use it to train generalized AI models. Google user data is transferred only as needed for authorized functionality, security, legal obligations, or with the owner's consent.

Storage and retention

Authorization credentials are stored separately from this public website. Retrieved information and generated outputs may persist in local files, task records, conversation history, backups and owner selected services. Retention depends on those stores and the owner's cleanup choices; there is no automatic universal deletion period. Private Google content and credentials must not be committed to this public site.

Control and deletion

The owner can stop using the integration and revoke its Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized access but does not automatically delete previously saved copies. The owner can delete local records and connected service outputs, and use provider controls for data retained by those providers. For assistance, use the developer contact displayed on the Google authorization screen. Do not submit sensitive information through public repository discussions.

Security and website hosting

Account permissions, credential separation and scoped operational controls are used to protect access. No system can guarantee absolute security. This informational site is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process visitor connection information under its own privacy policy. These pages contain no analytics scripts, forms or Google login functionality.

Google API policy

Personal Workspace Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes

This notice will be updated when the integration's data practices change.
